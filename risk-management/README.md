# Risk Assessment and Register

[Resource library](../README.md)

Describe risk as: because of a cause or condition, an event may occur, resulting in a business or stakeholder impact.

## Assessment workflow

1. Define scope, objectives, affected stakeholders, and assessment horizon.
2. Record inherent exposure before the assessed controls.
3. Review control evidence and known gaps.
4. Evaluate residual exposure using the same approved criteria.
5. Select treatment: avoid, reduce, share, or accept. Sharing risk does not remove accountability.
6. Assign an owner, due date, authorized decision maker, and reassessment trigger.

## Rating criteria

Approve likelihood definitions for a stated time horizon and impact definitions covering financial loss, service disruption, legal obligations, and affected people. Record the method and version used.

If using ordinal scores, document category boundaries and escalation rules. A multiplied score is a prioritization aid, not a quantified probability or financial loss estimate. Do not reduce residual ratings without supporting evidence. Escalate risks beyond delegated appetite and record uncertainty rather than assigning false precision.

Link acceptance to a time-bound [exception record](../policy-management/EXCEPTION-REGISTER-TEMPLATE.md) where applicable. Reassess on material changes, control failures, incidents, obligation changes, or the scheduled review date.
