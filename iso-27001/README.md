# ISO 27001 Governance

[Resource library](../README.md)

This independent resource supports oversight of an information security management system (ISMS). It does not reproduce ISO clauses or Annex A, prescribe technical controls, or provide a complete certification checklist.

Reference baseline: ISO/IEC 27001:2022. Confirm the applicable edition and amendments using the [official ISO source](https://www.iso.org/standard/27001) and the organization's authorized copy.

## Governance records

| Record | Purpose | Accountable role to assign |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Context, interested parties, and scope | Define ISMS boundaries, dependencies, and relevant expectations | ISMS owner |
| Policy and objectives | Establish direction and measurable intended outcomes | Leadership |
| Risk assessment and treatment plan | Record risk decisions and treatment responsibilities | Risk owners |
| Statement of Applicability | Record necessary controls, inclusion rationale, implementation status, and justification for excluded Annex A controls | ISMS owner with control owners |
| Control and evidence register | Track responsibilities and evidence of operation | Control owners |
| Internal audit plan and results | Provide objective evaluation against defined criteria | Audit lead |
| Management review record | Capture performance review and leadership decisions | Leadership |
| Nonconformity and corrective action log | Address causes and verify corrective action effectiveness | Action owners |

Use the official standard to determine mandatory content. This table is a governance aid, not an exhaustive requirements mapping.

Plan inputs against the applicable standard and local governance arrangements. Document improvement decisions, required changes, and resources. Track previous actions through completion.

## Oversight checklist

- [ ] Scope, policy, objectives, and responsibilities are approved.
- [ ] Risk criteria and treatment decisions are documented.
- [ ] The Statement of Applicability is consistent with treatment decisions and actual control status.
- [ ] Internal audit responsibilities support objectivity and impartiality.
- [ ] Management review considers evidence and records resulting actions.
- [ ] Corrective actions address causes and undergo effectiveness review.
- [ ] Material scope, obligation, and risk changes trigger reassessment.
