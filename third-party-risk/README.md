## Due diligence and decision

1. Classify criticality using business dependency, data sensitivity, substitutability, and potential stakeholder harm.
2. Establish applicable contractual and regulatory obligations with the relevant specialist.
3. Review assurance scope, covered services, reporting period, exceptions, subcontractors, and customer responsibilities.
4. Assess gaps and concentration risk; record treatment, accountable owners, and decision authority.
5. Agree oversight, notification, evidence-access, renewal, and exit expectations in the appropriate contract process.
6. Approve, approve with conditions, defer, or reject; preserve rationale.

A supplier certificate or report does not establish assurance over every service or transfer customer responsibilities.

## Ongoing oversight

Set review frequency by approved risk criteria and external obligations. Reassess after material service, ownership, location, subcontractor, or assurance changes, incidents, or persistent performance failures.
