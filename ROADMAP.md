These are planned additions, not currently validated crosswalks or promised release dates. Prioritize usefulness, accuracy, and reviewer feedback over artifact count.

## Scope gate

Every addition must support a GRC decision, accountable obligation, or assurance activity. Engineering implementation, Secure SDLC, security tooling, and AI security testing remain outside scope. All examples must be fictional and free of proprietary or confidential material.
