## Community priorities

Community feedback is particularly useful on:

1. controls that are difficult to implement in real engineering environments;
2. governance requirements that are routinely interpreted ambiguously;
3. areas where GRC teams ask for evidence that engineering teams cannot reasonably produce;
4. AI/agentic security risks where existing governance practices do not map cleanly to technical architecture;
5. opportunities to automate control validation or evidence generation.

If you have practical experience with any of these areas, please open an issue or propose a change.

## Versioning philosophy

Artifacts will evolve through visible commits and community review. Substantive methodology changes should explain:

- the problem being addressed;
- why the existing approach is insufficient;
- the proposed change;
- practical implications for implementers;
- supporting references or implementation experience where available.
