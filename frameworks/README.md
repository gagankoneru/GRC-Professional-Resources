# Regulatory Obligations and Framework Mappings

[Resource library](../README.md)

Maintain an obligations register before mapping controls. Applicability depends on the organization, activities, locations, contracts, and effective dates.

These are blank templates, not a validated crosswalk. A similar theme does not establish equivalence. A mapping does not prove applicability, effective operation, or compliance.

## Review method

1. Obtain the authoritative source and record its version.
2. Determine applicability and document exclusions with rationale.
3. Map individual obligations to control objectives and scope.
4. Identify unmapped elements and separate evidence requirements.
5. Obtain specialist review before relying on the mapping.
6. Reassess when either the obligation or control changes.

Reuse evidence only when scope, period, population, and assessment purpose align. Respect source licensing; reference restricted requirements without reproducing them.
