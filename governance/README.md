# Governance Operating Model

[Resource library](../README.md)

Use this model to define how risk and compliance decisions are made and challenged.

| Role | Accountability | Decision or record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Governing body / delegated committee | Oversight of risk appetite and material exposure | Approved appetite, escalation decisions |
| Executive sponsor | Resources and program priorities | Budget and unresolved issue decisions |
| Business risk owner | Risk treatment and authorized acceptance | Risk decision record |
| Control owner | Control performance and remediation | Control attestation and action plan |
| GRC function | Methodology, challenge, obligation tracking | Risk and compliance reporting |
| Independent assurance | Objective evaluation within its mandate | Findings and assurance conclusions |

Adapt roles to the organization. Avoid self-approval of exceptions and self-review of assurance work; document alternative safeguards when roles must overlap.
