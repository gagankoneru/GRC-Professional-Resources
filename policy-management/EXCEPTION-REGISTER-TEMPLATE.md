## Workflow

Requested → assessed → approved or rejected → monitored → closed or expired.

Only activate an exception after authorized approval. Record rejected and withdrawn requests for traceability. At expiry, escalate any unresolved deviation and mark approval as expired. Track the underlying nonconformity until verified closure; do not silently reset its age. Repeated renewals require review of the underlying policy, resources, or control design.
