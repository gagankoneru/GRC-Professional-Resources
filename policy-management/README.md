# Policy and Exception Management

[Resource library](../README.md)

## Policy lifecycle

1. Identify the obligation or risk and intended audience.
2. Assign a policy owner and obtain stakeholder review.
3. Approve through documented authority.
4. Publish the controlled version and communicate responsibilities.
5. Track acknowledgements where required and assess implementation evidence.
6. Review after material changes or by the approved review date.
7. Archive superseded versions while preserving decision history.

## Exceptions

Use the [exception template](EXCEPTION-REGISTER-TEMPLATE.md) when a policy or control obligation cannot be met. Review the risk, legal/contractual constraints, proposed safeguards, and delegated authority before deciding.

An internal risk acceptance does not waive an external requirement or establish compliance. Route such cases to the accountable compliance/legal specialist and relevant external authority where applicable. Renewals require reassessment and fresh approval; expiry is not automatic renewal.
