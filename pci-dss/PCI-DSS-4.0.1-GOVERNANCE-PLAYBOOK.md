# PCI DSS v4.0.1 Governance Playbook

[PCI DSS resources](README.md)

A governance workflow for scope, accountability, obligations, evidence, findings, and management reporting. Technical configurations and security engineering procedures are outside scope.

## 1. Establish accountability

Appoint an executive sponsor, PCI program owner, business/process owners, control owners, evidence custodians, supplier relationship owners, and assessment coordinator. Record delegated approval authority and escalation routes.

Use the [control owner register](CONTROL-OWNER-REGISTER-TEMPLATE.md). GRC coordinates and challenges; ownership remains with those accountable for each control outcome.

## 2. Govern scope

Use the [scoping questionnaire](SCOPING-QUESTIONNAIRE.md) to maintain a documented scope decision and supporting specialist evidence. Record payment channels, account-data handling, relevant dependencies, service providers, exclusions, and unresolved questions.

Have qualified specialists substantiate technical scope assumptions. Record the reviewer, date, and reassessment triggers. Outsourcing or segmentation claims require supported evaluation; they are not automatic scope exclusions.

## 3. Maintain obligation coverage

Record exact applicable requirement references, source version, scope, responsible owner, control ID, expected evidence, and assessment approach. Resolve applicability questions using official material and the relevant assessor or compliance-program authority.

Derive required frequencies from applicable requirements and authorized analyses where permitted. Management reporting schedules do not replace required control or review frequencies.

## 4. Plan assessment-period evidence

Use the [evidence catalogue](PCI-DSS-EVIDENCE-CATALOGUE.md). Assign evidence requests to owners and track population, period, completeness, quality, and missing records. A current document may not demonstrate operation throughout an assessment period.

## 5. Oversee service providers

Maintain service-specific responsibility allocations, contractual commitments, relevant assurance scope and period, unresolved gaps, and customer-retained responsibilities. Verify the evidence covers the service used. Track reassessment dates and material supplier changes.

## 6. Manage gaps and risk decisions

Track control failures and assessment findings with severity rationale, accountable owner, corrective action, due date, and verification evidence.

An internal exception or risk acceptance does not satisfy or waive PCI DSS. Do not label ordinary interim safeguards as validated compensating controls. Where an official alternative approach is relevant, verify eligibility, documentation, and assessment requirements against PCI SSC material with qualified support. Keep unresolved compliance gaps visible.

## 7. Report and reassess

Report scope questions, unassigned controls, evidence gaps, overdue findings, and supplier assurance gaps using defined populations and as-of dates. Agree management review cadence and urgent escalation criteria.

Reassess after material business, payment-channel, service-provider, or environment changes, and at required intervals. Track decisions through verified completion.

## Assessment readiness

- [ ] Scope, exclusions, and supporting evidence reviewed.
- [ ] Applicable requirements and owners recorded.
- [ ] Required activities and frequencies tracked.
- [ ] Evidence quality and assessment-period coverage reviewed.
- [ ] Service-provider responsibilities reconciled.
- [ ] Gaps, corrective actions, and limitations disclosed.
- [ ] Assessment coordination and management sign-off responsibilities assigned.

Authoritative reference: [PCI SSC Document Library](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/document_library/). This playbook is independent guidance, not a complete requirements checklist or compliance determination.
