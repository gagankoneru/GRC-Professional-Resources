# GRC Metrics and KRIs

[Resource library](../README.md)

Each metric needs an owner, reporting period, population, source, calculation, threshold rationale, and management response. The catalogue below suggests measures; it sets no universal compliance thresholds.

| Measure | Calculation | Type | Decision supported |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Control ownership coverage | Controls with a current named accountable owner / in-scope controls × 100 | KPI | Assign ownership gaps |
| Evidence acceptance rate | Evidence items accepted after quality review / items due in period × 100 | KPI | Resolve readiness gaps |
| Overdue high-risk treatments | Count of open high-risk actions past approved due date | KRI | Escalate residual exposure |
| Expired unresolved exceptions | Count of unresolved deviations whose approval expired | KRI | Obtain treatment decisions |
| Overdue policy reviews | Active policies past review due date / active policies × 100 | KPI | Restore policy oversight |
| Critical supplier assurance gaps | Critical suppliers missing required current assurance / critical suppliers × 100 | KRI | Reassess supplier reliance |
| Repeat findings | Repeat findings / findings raised in period × 100 | KRI | Address ineffective corrective action |
| Verified closure rate | Findings verified closed by due date / findings due in period × 100 | KPI | Assess remediation performance |

Report a zero denominator as not applicable, with rationale. Report unknown data separately; never convert missing evidence to success. Preserve original due dates and approved changes so extensions cannot hide overdue work.

Approve thresholds against appetite and obligations. Explain changes in scope, population, severity, and data collection before comparing periods. Counts or green dashboards alone cannot support a compliance conclusion.
